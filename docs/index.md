# Brewery Art Walk

The official-style guide app to **the Brewery**, the largest live-work
art colony in the world — tucked into a historic former brewery in
Lincoln Heights, Los Angeles.

* [Privacy Policy](./privacy/)
* [Support](./support/)
* [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Mattkillsyou/ArtWalk)

---

## What it does

* Find artists by name, medium, or category
* See every studio on a clean campus map
* Get directions on foot — the map shows where you are in real time
* Save your favorite artists
* Browse offline once you've opened the app once

## Why it exists

The Brewery campus is a maze. This app makes it easy to walk it.

No accounts. No ads. No tracking. Open source.
