# Brewery Art Walk — Support

Need help with the app? Found a wrong address, a missing artist, or a
bug? Want to suggest a feature?

**Email:** hello@breweryartwalk.app
We usually reply within a few days.

## Common questions

### The app says "Location permission denied" when I tap Find me

iOS → Settings → Brewery Art Walk → Location → set to **While Using
the App**.

### My favorites disappeared after reinstalling

That's expected — favorites are stored on the device, not in the
cloud. Reinstalling the app clears them.

### The map looks blurry / a building is in the wrong spot

Email us with a screenshot — we'll fix it in the next release.

### Is the app free?

Yes, completely. No ads, no in-app purchases, no upsells.

### When is the next Brewery Art Walk?

The Brewery Art Walk happens twice a year, usually fall and spring.
Check **breweryartwalk.com** for current dates.
